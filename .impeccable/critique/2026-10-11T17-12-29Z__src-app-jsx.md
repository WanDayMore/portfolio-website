### Priority Issues

#### [P0] Trapped Modal Focus & Missing Escape/Backdrop Dismissal
- **What:** In `ProjectModal.jsx` and `ResumeModal.jsx`, pressing `Escape` does not close the dialog, and clicking the dark backdrop overlay fails to dismiss it.
- **Why it matters:** Breaks basic web usability (Nielsen H3) and WCAG modal criteria. Recruiters on laptops or mobile devices are trapped until locating the small 'X' button.
- **Fix:** Add `useEffect` listening for `e.key === 'Escape'`, attach `onClick={onClose}` to the backdrop overlay, and apply `e.stopPropagation()` on the dialog card.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable polish`

#### [P1] Disorienting "Fake Submission" in Contact Section
- **What:** The contact form appears to be a functional web form, but clicking "Send Message" triggers `window.location.href = mailto:...` and immediately flips to a triumphant "Inquiry Initialized!" success card.
- **Why it matters:** On machines without a configured default mail client, nothing happens externally while the site claims success.
- **Fix:** Replace the illusionary form with a transparent "Direct Reach Hub" featuring one-click email copying, pre-formatted subject templates, and direct LinkedIn messaging.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify`

#### [P1] Broken Design Token Discipline & Color Drift
- **What:** The interface violates the "Blueprint Indigo ≤10%" rule by arbitrarily mixing Tailwind `blue-600`, `blue-500`, `blue-400`, `indigo-400`, `indigo-500`, and `indigo-600`. In `ContactSection`, input focus states alternate between indigo and blue across consecutive rows.
- **Why it matters:** Inconsistent color tokens make the interface look cobbled together rather than engineered with the precision expected of an enterprise full-stack engineer.
- **Fix:** Standardize all primary interactive elements to `indigo-600` (`#6366f1`), use `emerald-400` strictly for live status/telemetry, and purge raw blue tokens.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable typeset`

#### [P2] Visual Redundancy & Competing Focal Draws in Hero
- **What:** The Hero splits into two heavy columns: the left has the headline, role, bio, and 3 buttons; the right has a floating card repeating his headshot, name, title, school, 7 skills, and a bash quote.
- **Why it matters:** High cognitive load. Recruiters with 45 seconds to scan are confused by redundant information and are distracted by the entry-level `$ echo "Philosophy"` terminal snippet.
- **Fix:** Unify the Hero: integrate the authentic headshot cleanly alongside the core narrative, remove the duplicate right-hand card, and replace the bash quote with a live telemetry or architectural summary badge.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout`

#### [P2] Deceptive Action Affordances in Project Lightbox
- **What:** `ProjectModal` displays two prominent primary buttons: "View Repository" and "Project Details". Both buttons link to `https://github.com/WanDayMore`.
- **Why it matters:** Violates the principle of least surprise. A technical director clicking "Project Details" expecting an architectural whitepaper or live demo lands on the exact same GitHub profile.
- **Fix:** Conditionally render a second CTA only if a distinct live URL exists; otherwise, present a single clear "Inspect Source on GitHub" button.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify`
