## Product Principles

1. **Evidence Over Assertion:** Every claim of skill is substantiated with real screenshots, architectural notes, and verifiable implementation details.
2. **Scannability First:** Critical signals (experience, stack, education, contact, status) are immediately legible within 5 seconds of viewport scanning.
3. **Clarity Without Clutter:** Clean visual hierarchy, purposeful typography, and fluid micro-interactions that elevate the work rather than competing with it.
4. **Frictionless Handoff:** Viewing the resume, inspecting GitHub source code, copying contact information, or sending a direct message requires at most one click.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Semantic HTML tags for navigation, sections, articles, and headings.
- Keyboard-accessible dialog modals with escape key handling and backdrop dismissals.
- Responsive reflow supporting mobile, tablet, and widescreen viewports without horizontal clipping.
